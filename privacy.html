Privacy Policy

Your privacy is of utmost importance to us. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it.

schedule Last updated: 2026-09-02

schedule ~5 min read
01

Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Email address, name, and authentication tokens (via Google or GitHub).
  • Usage Data: Queries, temporary uploaded files, response generations, and usage patterns to improve our AI models and user experience.
  • Device & Browser Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, and referring pages — used to diagnose issues and prevent abuse.
  • Cookies: Essential cookies to maintain your session and preferences. You can disable cookies, but some features may not function properly.
02

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the AXELR AI service
  • To personalise your experience, including workspace and model preferences
  • To monitor usage patterns, enforce rate limits, and prevent fraudulent or abusive activities
  • To communicate with you about service updates, security alerts, and support messages
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service
03

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for their marketing purposes. We may share your data with:

  • AI Providers: Queries may be processed by third-party AI providers (e.g., Google, Groq, OpenRouter) to generate responses. These providers process data only for fulfilling your requests.
  • Service Providers: Cloud infrastructure (e.g., MongoDB, Redis) hosting the platform. Limited access solely for operational purposes.
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law or in response to valid legal requests.
04

Data Retention

We retain your account information and chat history for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time, which will permanently remove your data from our systems. We may retain aggregated, anonymised data for analytical purposes.

05

Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

verified_user
Our commitment: We never train public models on your private data. Enterprise plans add zero-retention guarantees.
06

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You can manage your account settings directly or contact us at support@axelr.in for assistance. We will respond to your request within a reasonable timeframe.

07

Children's Privacy

AXELR AI is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware of such data, we will delete it promptly.

08

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or through the platform. Your continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

09

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@axelr.in.
